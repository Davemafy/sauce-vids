## Honest creative defects and next choice

- The model **selected a real useful local action**, but the first independent critic escalated an illustrative `build.py` marketing terminal into unsupported evidence. A validator preventing fake action claims was essential.
- Cause→effect now visibly real; however `fill` applies a whole-text update abruptly rather than a believable human type-in. Do not invent smooth typing in postproduction or pretend a full cloud workspace ran.
- Original page includes irrelevant footer/CTA below the editor; fallback zoom toward right iframe trims code context. Top navigation and lower labels become partially cropped, and 390px viewers cannot reliably read small labels. The viewer understands a changed heading, **not** a high-value differentiated SaaS workflow.
- The model still cannot reliably compile temporal visual JSON, even when it knows why the action is interesting. Renderer guards cannot be relaxed to make the film green. This **does not** support claims of general autonomous premium filmmaking.
- Against recorded Screen Studio pushes (~1.62× with action-driven release), this is a short, relatively mechanical camera move on a very ordinary browser sandbox. **No premium acceptance**; recommendation is a richer genuinely interactive public/safe real product source or owner-authorized workflow with high-value state changes, plus per-frame real video criticism, not more static text replacement or decorative effects.
