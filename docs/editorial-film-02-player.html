Why this one works

Every shot has a reason. The camera starts with context, approaches the selected values, follows the formatting action and holds the result long enough to understand it.

The voice is generated locally. Caption emphasis is aligned to the recorded speech. The audio mix is mastered to approximately −16 LUFS, rather than leaving narration buried under music.

The actual sequence

  1. Present the unformatted expenses 0:00–0:03
  2. Select five real spreadsheet cells 0:03–0:04
  3. Apply the native currency button 0:04–0:05
  4. Hold the verified new values 0:05–0:08
  5. Return to the complete sheet 0:08–0:11
