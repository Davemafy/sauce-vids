## Reusable source-true engine implementation

`scripts/source_mouse_spring_camera.py` is a deterministic, stateless-at-sampling, causal spring follower:

1. Requires the original `pointer_samples` of the exact hashed browser manifest, at least three strictly increasing finite source-clock samples and identical native source `capture_rect`. No samples, changed geometry, missing evidence, duplicate timestamps, unknown style or unverified event → **hard reject**.
2. Follows only explicit, verified `eventIndices` in a finite source-clock window around real interactions. Gaps over **0.20s** in original cursor samples remain a *held observed point*, never a fabricated continuous mouse route; the camera is free to return to rest after the meaningful result.
3. A bounded source-normalized **dead zone** ignores tiny cursor shifts; `maxOffset` is capped at **7.5%** of the output region even under untrusted JSON. Uses the **exact analytical solution of a critically damped second-order spring**, time-sampled at native source fps; default `responseSeconds=0.39` (~95% settle). No frame-order dependence, random wobble or overshoot.
4. Reference-measured camera ease `reference_ease_out` reaches ~61% of a scheduled move in the first 30% of its duration, ~96% by 70%, then settles quietly. All endpoints remain fixed and native event timings unchanged.

`scripts/spatial_studio_stage.py`: opt-in `studioStage.motion.profile="reference-spring"` + `motion.follow={mode:"source-cursor-spring",eventIndices:[...]}` makes source camera center move with the real original pointer. Film EDL camera keys may additionally specify `viewScale:1..1.8` **only** with `motion.immersive=true`, on the existing `cloud_shadow_v1` stage. This makes the physical rounded browser actually grow beyond its distant floating card and later return, while the camera follows the measured cursor—not a low-quality zoom confined inside a static window. Compatible current-main classic/cinematic camera defaults stay unchanged.

`scripts/cloud_shadow_stage.py`: when and only when immersion is explicitly active, apply the same physical camera transform to the existing owner's blue halftone clouds, floating rounded matte, rim and existing soft shadows; separately project the **original real native browser video** through the resulting camera quad at output resolution (rather than enlarge a previously downscaled screenshot).
